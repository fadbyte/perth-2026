The coast is much cooler than Perth. Perth sits around 30°C by day while Albany and Esperance sit in the low 20s, with a sea breeze. Pack a jumper for the southern nights — 13–15°C — even in midsummer.

Daylight. Sunrise is around 04:50 and sunset around 19:20 in the south. A 09:00 departure is well clear of dawn; on the long legs, aim to be parked by 18:00 so you are never driving into dusk.

Rain is unlikely but not impossible — December is the driest month across the whole route.

Long-term December averages, not a forecast — a real forecast for these dates won't exist until about a week before. Check the Bureau of Meteorology (bom.gov.au) from around 6 Dec.
